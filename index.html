About

I am a PhD student in the Department of Physics at Bilkent University. My research lies at the intersection of condensed matter physics, strongly correlated quantum systems, topological phases of matter, and emerging physical machine learning approaches.

Research Interests

Fractional Quantum Hall Physics

Fractional quantum Hall states, fractional statistics, bosonic quantum Hall systems, and strongly correlated phases.

Rotating Bosonic Systems

Rapidly rotating Bose gases, collapse phenomena, many-body effects, and exotic correlated ground states.

Topological Lattice Models

Fractional Chern insulators, flat-band physics, Hofstadter-type models, and topological band structures.

Physical Neural Networks

Analog computation, physical machine learning, and hardware-inspired approaches to learning in complex systems.
